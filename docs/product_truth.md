## Pricing & packaging — one flat, all-inclusive monthly rate per tier

Three tiers, each a single predictable **monthly flat rate that covers EVERYTHING**.
Nothing for the customer to procure, set up, or manage.

| Tier | Price/mo |
|---|---|
| **Starter** | **$3,500** |
| **Scale** (most popular) | **$5,500** |
| **Advanced** | **$7,000** |

3-month opt-out · live in ~5 weeks · forward-deployed GTM AI engineer included.

**Included in the flat rate at every tier — the customer procures nothing:**

  **done-for-you service**, using best-in-class models for each part of the

  sequencing** — fully managed by EverWorker. Included.
- CRM integration · single-tenant platform.

Tiers differ by **signal/enrichment volume and advanced capabilities** (more
deanonymization / technographic / hiring signals, enrichment volume, etc.), **not**
by what the customer assembles or procures.

## Models — done-for-you, private, no "bring your own"

EverWorker acquires the model endpoints as a **done-for-you service**. They are
**fully private — no training, no retention** on customer data. EverWorker uses a
**mix of high-performance models from OpenAI and Anthropic, plus smaller OpenAI and
Anthropic models** where appropriate, following best practices developed from
architecting and testing multi-agent GTM systems. **The customer never has to think
about models.**

> **FALSE — never say:** "you power it with your own endpoints," "bring your own
> model," "your choice of Claude / GPT / Perplexity / Bedrock," "you keep full model
> control." Models are EverWorker's done-for-you, private, mixed-model service — the
> customer does not bring or use their own endpoints.
>
> **True framing for a model / lock-in question:** "You don't manage models at all —
> we procure private, no-train/no-retain endpoints and run a best-in-class mix of
> OpenAI and Anthropic models tuned per part of the system. It's handled, and it's
> always current — nothing for you to procure or swap."

## Infrastructure providers — NEVER disclosed to the customer

The customer **never needs to know or worry about** which providers power email,
sending, or LinkedIn. It is all included in the flat rate and fully managed by
EverWorker. **Scripts must NOT name any provider.** Say "our managed email
infrastructure," "our built-in sequencer," "our managed LinkedIn infrastructure" —
EverWorker-branded, included, done-for-you.

> **Never say in a customer-facing script:** "ScaledMail," "Email Bison," "HeyReach,"
> "Prospeo," "People Data Labs," or any third-party provider name, account counts, or
> "we run on <provider>." Frame it all as EverWorker's managed, included infrastructure.

### Internal knowledge — for agents' understanding ONLY, never customer-facing
So agents understand what reps mean when recognizing call content (do **not** put any
of these names into a script):
- Email sequencing = **whitelabeled Email Bison**, EverWorker-branded; each customer
  gets an **isolated workspace** with full visibility into their AI SDR's sequences
  and activity, but nothing to operate.
- LinkedIn sequencing = **whitelabeled HeyReach**, same isolated-workspace model.
- Email sending capacity = handled on the back end via **ScaledMail**; the customer
  never sees or interacts with it — EverWorker just secures the capacity.
- LinkedIn account rental = **real, ID-validated profiles**, handled by EverWorker;
  the customer never sees or interacts with it.

  sequencing — all handled by EverWorker, branded EverWorker.
